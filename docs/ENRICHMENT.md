# Object enrichment v1.1 — offline candidate

Preserve MAIN ART DIRECTION byte-for-byte and independently hashed. Enrich meaning, construction and storytelling, not grain, pores, random marks or extra gloss. Separate identity, contents, decoration, attached parts, loose accessories, material and lighting.

Only explicit user-authored restrictions are hard locks. Prior model-authored empty/plain choices are editable defaults. Latest user instructions override them. Do not parse historical phrases such as “unlike the earlier empty mug” into operative locks. Require structured provenance, and flag contradictory user constraints for review.

Use variable low/medium/high detail budgets; prefer a few meaningful readable groups. High is not maximum density. Contents remain subordinate to their container; motifs follow the surface. Never copy flowers/tea/ornaments from a style example onto every subject. Tools may need only functional detail. Bowl/basket content requirements and cushion decoration may be user requirements; exact fruit/bread/motif choices remain declared assumptions.

All parts share one weak-perspective camera, coherent axes, openings, thicknesses, attachments and elevation. Keep organic asymmetry where appropriate. Separate production category from asset stage: C1 isolated subject; C2 coherent compound subject on a plain full-width plane; C3 meaningful developed full-width surface; C4 whole scene. C2 composes a separate simple full-width gradient plane. C3 generates object plus developed meaningful full-width surface together as a transparent assembly; its background remains procedural. Do not invent pedestals or silently turn C1 into a scene.

The compiler checks structured locks/provenance/category/stage/AD hash, then returns an offline candidate and explicit unverified semantic/visual checks. It does not prove quality by string checks. No image calls, retries, notebook edits or policy learning are triggered. Existing experiment records remain unchanged.
